# What's New — draft (≤ 4000 characters)

Clarity — next release: practice that adapts to the person

NEW FOR EVERYDAY LIFE
• Today card: the day, date and time of day on your Home screen, in your language, to help with orientation. Caregivers can turn it off.
• Number Skills: hear a time, price, phone number, date or count, then pick it or type it. Caregivers can add a phone number to practice; it stays on the device.
• Remember It: practice remembering something important with gradually longer pauses. Caregivers add up to three questions and answers; they never leave the device.
• Visual Scanning: find-the-target grids, with an in-order practice mode and an optional flashing marker on the left edge.

SUPPORTS FOR CAREGIVERS
• Answer choices: show 2, 3 or 4 options on multiple-choice questions.
• Word-finding hints: a gentle three-step hint (meaning, first letter, show the answer). Hinted answers are kept separate from first-try answers.
• A second daily reminder, for example after lunch.
• Practice Supports card in Therapy Settings groups these controls.

ENGLISH FIRST
• Reading Passages with read-aloud, look-back and a hint that highlights the right sentence, plus a self-rating of understanding.
• Conversation Starters: picture-free cards for two people to talk together.

FIXES
• Apple TV: long questions are no longer cut off.
• Corrected many exercises in Amharic, Arabic, Armenian, Farsi, Gujarati, Hindi, Korean, Portuguese, Punjabi and Vietnamese (wrong answer formats, repeated questions, stray letters).
• Updated a population question so it stays accurate.

STILL PRIVATE
Clarity works offline, with no accounts and no tracking. Research export stays anonymous and never includes names, phone numbers or personal memory questions.

Clarity is a practice tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.
