Clarity for caregivers

You do so much. Clarity is here to help, and to look after you too.

Whether you are a spouse, a son or daughter, a friend, or a professional, caring for someone with aphasia or early dementia is real, daily work. This page walks through everything in Clarity that is built for you.

Sit down together

Practice that needs no preparation

You should not need a lesson plan or a spare hour. Clarity is designed so that you can sit down next to the person you care for and start in seconds.

🎲

Surprise Me

One tap launches a random activity. When energy is low and deciding what to do feels like one task too many, this removes the decision.

⏱️

Five questions per session

Short by design, so a session fits into a good ten minutes and never turns into a wall of fatigue. A consistent daily habit matters more than a long session.

📺

Take turns on Apple TV

Two-player mode lets you and the person you care for take turns on the same screen with one remote, a natural way to practise side by side.

💬

Conversation cards

Topic cards for two people to talk together, plus short reading passages with read-aloud, look-back and hints. Available in English first.

🌍

In the language that feels like home

Clarity is available in 18 languages, so a family can practise in the language the person grew up with. Switch languages in one tap from the Profile tab.

🔠

Large text and VoiceOver

Text follows your Dynamic Type setting, and key screens are labelled for VoiceOver.

Caregiver Mode

Your own PIN-protected dashboard

Caregiver Mode keeps the tools meant for you away from the practice screens. You set your own 4-digit PIN, stored securely on the device, and everything below sits behind it.

  • Progress, in detailA full session history you can filter, a simple accuracy trend, and a PDF progress report built on the device, ready to share with a therapist or doctor.
  • Insights in plain languageShort observations drawn from the practice history that already exists. Insights are framed around the goal: recovery-oriented for stroke and brain injury, stability-oriented for progressive conditions. They read from existing data and collect nothing new.
  • Weekly goalChoose a realistic target of 3, 5, 7, 10 or 14 sessions a week. The goal also shows on the Home Screen widget.
  • Therapy notesTimestamped notes for you and the care team: what worked, what was hard, what to tell the therapist next week. Notes are stored in the device's secure Keychain.
  • Baseline AssessmentA short assessment you can run again at any time, to reset the starting point as things change.
  • Flagged questionsAny question can be flagged from inside an exercise. Caregiver Mode lists what has been flagged, with no personal information attached, so it can be reviewed and passed on.
  • Reminders that fit your dayA daily practice reminder, an optional second one (after lunch, for example), a gentle nudge if the evening arrives without a session, and a welcome-back message after a few days away.
Tune the practice

Make it fit the person, not the other way round

Every person is in a different place, and that place changes from day to day. These settings, all in Caregiver Mode, let you adjust how Clarity presents things.

🎚️

Adaptive difficulty, with a manual override

Clarity quietly adjusts difficulty as the person improves, with no numbers for them to track. If you know better, you can set difficulty by exercise yourself.

🌱

Recovery or Maintenance framing

Choose the orientation that matches the situation, and expect day-to-day fluctuation. A good day and a hard day are both normal.

💡

Hints and fewer choices

Step-by-step word-finding hints, and a setting for 2, 3 or 4 answer choices, so questions feel achievable rather than overwhelming.

🧠

Remember It

Set up to three personal questions (a family member's name, an address, a medication time) practised with gradually longer pauses. These stay on the device and are never exported.

🔢

Number skills

Hear a time, a price, a phone number or a date, then pick it or type it. The person can practise their own phone number, kept only on the device.

📅

Today card

The day, date and time of day on the Home screen, in the person's language, to help with orientation.

🔍

Visual scanning

Find-the-target grids with an in-order practice mode and a left-edge marker.

📱

Home Screen widget

The current streak, today's recommended exercise and the weekly goal, right on the iPhone Home Screen. One tap starts the activity.

Private by design

What you write stays with you

Fully offline

No account, no network calls, no third-party code. Notes, targets and progress never leave the device.

Personal details are separate

Names, notes, phone numbers and Remember It targets live in the device's secure Keychain. They are never part of any research export.

Anonymous research export

If a therapist or researcher asks for practice data, the export contains no personal information.

For you

And then there is you

Caregivers give a great deal and often hear very little in return. Burnout is common, and it is not a sign that you are doing anything wrong. So inside Caregiver Mode, Clarity has a quiet space that has nothing to do with exercises, scores or the person you care for. It is simply for you.

🤍

"How are YOU doing?"

A simple check-in with four honest answers, from doing well to running on empty, each with a kind reply. If things feel like too much, it points you to people you can talk to.

🌬️

Breathe

A one-minute guided breathing exercise: a slowly expanding circle and nothing else to do. It respects the Reduce Motion setting.

🍃

Ground yourself

A gentle five-senses exercise that takes about two minutes, for the moments when everything feels like too much.

✉️

A small daily note

One short, honest message a day: the kind of thing caregivers deserve to hear and rarely do.

🧭

Where to find support

Links and phone numbers for caregiver organizations, including Family Caregiver Alliance, Caregiver Action Network, the Alzheimer's Association helpline, the Eldercare Locator, and the 988 Lifeline.

🔒

Nothing is recorded

Your check-in answer is never saved, tracked, exported or shown to anyone, including the person you care for. It disappears when you close the screen.

This space is a moment of support, not treatment, and it is not a substitute for professional care. If you are struggling, please reach out to a doctor or a mental health professional. In the US you can call or text 988 at any time. The support links and the "For you" space are currently in English, and are US-focused.
