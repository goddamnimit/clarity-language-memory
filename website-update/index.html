What makes Clarity different

Built around real lives, not textbooks

📵

Fully offline, truly private

Every word, every exercise, every session runs entirely on-device. Zero network calls, zero third-party code — your data never leaves your phone. Reliable in hospitals, care homes, and anywhere connectivity is limited.

🌍

16 languages, one app

English, Spanish, Hindi, Gujarati, Chinese, Farsi, Korean, Vietnamese, Arabic, Portuguese, Tagalog, Punjabi, Armenian, Japanese, French, and Amharic — each adapted for its community, with native speaker review in progress. Switch languages in one tap from the Profile tab. Full right-to-left support for Farsi and Arabic.

🎯

30+ exercises per language

Covering language skills, cognition, and functional daily living. Sessions are short, varied, and gently adaptive so fatigue never becomes a wall.

🎚️

Adapts as you improve

Clarity quietly adjusts difficulty across all 16 languages — no settings to fuss with, no numbers to track. A short Baseline Assessment on first run sets the right starting point automatically.

🎨

A new artwork every day

Every day Clarity displays a different public domain masterpiece — 75 works from the Met Museum and NASA, rotating automatically on both iPhone and Apple TV. A small, beautiful detail that makes opening the app feel like a daily event.

🛡️

Built for caregivers too

A PIN-protected Caregiver Mode gives family members and clinicians access to manual difficulty controls, therapy notes, goal setting, notification preferences, and a full exportable PDF progress report — without exposing any patient-facing settings.

⚡

5 questions per session

Brief by design. Five thoughtfully chosen questions per session respect the energy levels of recovering patients, while building a consistent daily habit without overwhelm.

🏆

Progress you can see

Streaks, badges, and a calendar view make practice feel rewarding. Seven achievement milestones mark real progress, from first session to 30-day streak.

🎲

Surprise Me

One tap on the Surprise Me button launches a random activity. Perfect for caregivers looking for a quick, low-friction session with a loved one.

📱

Today’s practice on your Home Screen

A Home Screen widget shows your streak, today’s recommended exercise, and your weekly goal — one tap to begin.

📺

Play together on Apple TV

Two people can take turns on one Apple TV with a single remote, in all 16 languages.

🧭

Recovery or Maintenance framing

Caregivers choose whether progress is read as recovery (stroke, TBI) or maintenance (progressive conditions), and can mark that day-to-day ups and downs are expected. Insights follow that choice.

📊

Caregiver insights

Plain-language trends by exercise type, with a clear note that Clarity is a practice tool, not a medical device.

🎙️

Speak your answers

On iPhone, open-ended questions can be answered by voice using on-device speech recognition, in the languages your iPhone supports.

🔠

Large text and VoiceOver

Text follows your Dynamic Type setting, and key screens are labelled for VoiceOver.

Who uses Clarity

For patients, families, and clinicians

01

People living with aphasia

Daily practice in short, manageable sessions, proven to help maintain and rebuild language and cognitive function after stroke or traumatic brain injury.

02

People with early FTD

Gentle cognitive exercises designed to support patients in the early stages of frontotemporal dementia, with content that is engaging without being frustrating.

03

Family caregivers

The Surprise Me button and quick 5-question sessions make it easy for a family member to sit down with a loved one and work through exercises together, without any preparation.

04

Speech-language clinicians

A free, offline supplemental tool for use between therapy sessions. Per-exercise difficulty controls and anonymous research data export make it easy to fit into a clinical workflow.

How it works

Four steps to start practicing

1

Set up your profile

Enter your name, choose your diagnosis, pick your preferred language, and select an avatar. A short Baseline Assessment then sets your starting difficulty automatically — or skip it and dive straight in.

2

Pick an activity

Browse by section — Language, Cognition, or Functional Skills — or hit Surprise Me to let the app choose for you.

3

Complete 5 questions

Answer at your own pace. Immediate, gentle feedback after each answer. See your score at the end and celebrate with confetti on a perfect round.

4

Build your streak

Come back tomorrow to extend your streak. Earn badges for milestones and watch your progress calendar fill in over time.
